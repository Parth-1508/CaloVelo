# CaloVelo Three-Theme Implementation Specification

> Handoff specification covering 18 checkpoints across 6 screen families and 3 color themes (`thermalDark`, `emeraldDark`, `emeraldLight`).

---

## 1. Theme Configuration Matrix

### Themes & Identifiers
1. **`thermalDark`**: Space Grotesk typography accents, coral/solar orange gradients (`#FF5733` to `#FF8D4D`), violet AI accents (`#8B5CF6`), sky cyan water (`#38BDF8`), deep thermal map styling.
2. **`emeraldDark`**: Inter typography, electric emerald start fills (`#00E676`), sunset orange scan fills (`#FF6D00`), deep slate background (`#0F172A`), surface `#1E293B`.
3. **`emeraldLight`**: Clean light background (`#F5F8F7`), white surfaces, slate ink (`#0F172A`), emerald/orange markers.

---

## 2. Screen Families & Fixtures

1. **Home**: Dual progress energy/hydration gauge (Emerald) or concentric bento cards (Thermal).
2. **Workout / Recovery**: Live tracking HUD with GPS accuracy, pace, distance, and RPE feedback sheet.
3. **AI Food Scanner**: CameraX viewfinder with bounding boxes (Paneer, Roti, Dal) and portion sliders.
4. **Training**: Weekly schedule, target metrics, and recovery intervals.
5. **Crew**: Sunset Striders event card, activity feed, and challenge progress.
6. **Nutrition Insights**: 7-day intake charts, average kcal, and recent meal journal.
